Phoenix (Re-Classic) model: johnwar
Source: https://www.hiveworkshop.com/threads/phoenix-re-classic.353829/
Author permission: "Anyone can edit my models as long as you give proper credits."

This map's EmberBird / QiLingPhoenix is an adapted model based on johnwar's
mesh, rig and mature animation work. New imagegen-authored body/head textures,
UV and tail-feather adjustments, fixed animation-index aliases and the separate
wild-unit origin variant were prepared for this map. The original model is not
claimed as original work by this project's author.

Referenced Warcraft III native textures, team color and native event assets
remain Blizzard assets. Their original files are not redistributed here.
MDX800/offline packaging checks are not a Warcraft III 1.27a runtime test.
